# Design System: Hostly

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Carta náutica"**

Hostly reads a rental season like a nautical chart of the coast. The brand's blue family becomes the chart's depth tints, lines are 1px hairlines, and numbers are soundings: how many nights a property has booked this month is written on a cell whose water is as deep as the month is full. The calendar is chart water, its month days carrying faint contour lines (isobaths) that run on across the cells; days of other months are shoal and recede. Every stay is traced across its nights in its property's ink.

The chart's title block is a Prussian top bar that carries the brand, the section tabs and the account. Below it, everything sits on pale chart paper in light mode or a deep navy night chart in dark mode, divided by hairlines and grid lines rather than lifted into boxes. The type is one variable family, Archivo: titles set wide like chart lettering, UI at normal width, secondary labels in italic like the names of waters, figures tabular. Density is that of a working tool: 14px body text, 36px controls, 6px corners.

The world refuses two things: the gray SaaS dashboard of stacked cards, and nautical costume. There is no compass rose, no anchor, no illustrated map; the chart lives only in the depth tints, the contour lines, the graticule and the soundings.

**Key Characteristics:**

- One blue family (Prussian, Sapphire, Celadon, Carolina, Azure), stepped into six depth tints that encode occupancy; sand is the one warm note.
- Eight property inks, fixed per property across every screen.
- A Prussian top bar as the brand field; no sidebar.
- Archivo Variable only: wide titles (118%), italic water labels, tabular figures. Never a serif.
- Hairlines, grid lines and tonal fields instead of shadows; contour lines and a graticule as the only texture.
- Motion that surfaces, fills like a tide and traces a course; nothing moves under reduced motion.

## Colors

A cool chart of blues stepped by depth, one warm sand, and eight distinct property inks reserved for telling properties apart.

### Primary

- **Sapphire** (`primary` in light): the action color on chart paper. Primary buttons, today's day number on the calendar, the selected-range ring, the active screen ring in the landing tour, the "Falta receber" figure, links.
- **Carolina** (`primary` in dark, `bar-active` in both, `ring` at night; on paper the ring is a step deeper, `#1a8fd2`, to keep 3:1): the action color at night, the focus ring everywhere, and the action on the Prussian bar (tab underline, the brand tile, "Criar conta").

### Secondary

- **Azure** (`secondary`, `muted`, `depth-1` in light): soft fills for secondary buttons and badges, the empty-state icon disc, the shallowest water, and the text on the bar (`bar-foreground`).
- **Chart Tint** (`accent`): hover fill for ghost and outline buttons, calendar day hover and the drag-selected range.

### Tertiary

- **Sand** (`sand` / `sand-foreground`): the land on the chart, the single warm color. It is declared as a token with a readable foreground; keep it rare.

### Depth tints

- **Depth 1 to 6** (`depth-1` ... `depth-6`, each with a `-foreground`): shallow Azure, two pale blues, Carolina, Sapphire, Prussian in light; in dark the ramp runs from night navy up to a bright pale blue, so the deepest water is always the most contrasted. `depthOf` maps a property's share of booked nights to a step: none is 1, up to 20% is 2, 40% is 3, 60% is 4, under 100% is 5, a full month is 6. Text selection also uses depth 4.

### Neutral

- **Chart Paper** (`background`): the light canvas, a faint blue white. **Night Chart** (`background-dark`) in dark.
- **Sheet White** (`card`, `popover`): the calendar's weekday header, popovers, the landing frame and quote card; deep navy (`card-dark`) at night.
- **Prussian Ink** (`foreground`): body text in light; Azure Ink at night.
- **Slate Blue** (`muted-foreground`): descriptions, captions, weekday labels, days outside the month.
- **Grid Line** (`grid-line`, equal to `border` in light): the color that shows through the 1px and 3px gaps between sounding cells and calendar days.
- **Shoal** (`shoal`): days outside the current month, receding.
- **Contour** (`contour`, a 7.5% Sapphire in light, 6% pale blue in dark): the ink of the contour lines and the graticule, never used as a fill.
- **Mist Line** (`border`): hairline dividers, decorative only.
- **Field Stroke** (`input`): the edge of every control (inputs, selects, radios, the date field). It keeps at least 3:1 against both the paper and the card in each theme (WCAG 1.4.11), so a field reads as a field; never lighten it back to a hairline.
- **Destructive, Success, Warning**: delete actions and errors, confirmations, the warning state. Each has a dark variant.

### Property inks

- **Property 1 to 8** (`property-1` ... `property-8`, each with a `-foreground`): sea blue, sea green, sand ochre, buoy red, violet, teal, orange, pale blue, with lighter values in dark. Assigned by the property's place in the name-sorted list, so a property keeps its ink on the calendar, the soundings key and the lists.

### Named Rules

**The Depth Rule.** Depth tints encode exactly one thing: how booked a property is. They fill sounding cells and nothing else that carries meaning; never use a depth step for status, emphasis or decoration.

**The Property Ink Rule.** Property inks identify a property (stay bars, the key dot on a sounding, list dots) and do nothing else, always with their paired foreground for text. Never use them for status or emphasis, and never let a property change ink between screens.

**The Bar Rule.** The top bar is the brand field: the `bar` family (Prussian in light, a deeper `bar-dark` at night), Azure text, muted tabs in `bar-muted`, and Carolina as the only action color on it. The auth panel and the public header are the same field. Nothing else in the app is Prussian-filled.

**The Contrast Rule.** Text never gets a translucent color or an `opacity-*` to look secondary: use `text-muted-foreground`, a lighter weight or the italic water label instead, so every text keeps 4.5:1 (3:1 when large) in both themes. Focus rings are drawn at full `ring` strength. Disabled controls are the only exception.

**The Theme Token Rule.** Colors come from theme classes (`bg-primary`, `text-muted-foreground`, `bg-depth-4`, `bg-bar`), never loose hex values in components, and every combination must work in light and dark.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo Variable at 118% width (`font-wide`), with `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif` fallback
**Body Font:** Archivo Variable at normal width
**Label Font:** Archivo Variable italic at 100% width (`font-water`); the system mono only for CPF/CNPJ in the brokers table

**Character:** One variable family carries the whole hierarchy by width, weight and scale. Titles stretch wide and bold like the lettering of a chart's title block, with slightly tight tracking (-0.015em); reading text stays at normal width; secondary explanatory labels lean into italic like the names of waters. Both cuts (`wdth` and `wdth-italic`) come from `@fontsource-variable/archivo`, no CDN.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (700, wide, 2.25rem → 3.75rem → 4.5rem, line-height 1.02, balanced): the landing hero headline only.
- **Headline** (700, wide, 1.875rem → 2.25rem, leading tight): page titles (`PageHeader`), landing section headings, auth titles and the auth panel statement; the landing CTA goes to 3rem.
- **Title** (600, wide, 1.5rem, line-height 1): the calendar's month or week heading, the "Falta receber" figure, the landing tour caption.
- **Sounding** (600, wide, 1.875rem in the app, 1.5rem on the landing and auth samples, line-height 1, tabular): the nights written on a sounding cell, followed by a water label ("de 31 noites").
- **Body** (400, 0.875rem): the app's working size for tables, forms and descriptions. Public pages read at 1rem with a 1.125rem lead and a `max-w-md` measure.
- **Label** (500, 0.75rem): stay bars (600), badges, and the calendar's weekday column headers, which are uppercase with wide tracking because they are column headings of a grid.
- **Water** (400 italic, 0.75rem–0.875rem): the explanatory line beside "Ocupação em …", the unit after a sounding, the figure caption on the auth panel.

### Named Rules

**The One Family Rule.** Archivo Variable is the only face. Hierarchy comes from width, weight and size; never introduce a second family, and never a serif.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Money, counts, dates and soundings use tabular figures (`tabular-nums`; tables get it globally).

## Layout

The logged-in app is a sticky top bar over a single content column. The bar is 56px tall on `md` and up, its contents capped at `max-w-7xl` (80rem) with 16px side padding on phones and 32px from `md`; on a phone the tabs drop to their own 44px row under the brand and scroll sideways. There is no sidebar. The main column uses the same `max-w-7xl` cap and padding, 24px top padding (32px from `md`) and 24px between page blocks. Pages open with the page header (wide title and description left, actions right, stacking on mobile). Editing happens in a right-side sheet, not a separate page.

The bookings page stacks, in order: header, property filter with the Calendário/Lista switch, the occupancy soundings, then the calendar or the list. Soundings form an auto-fit grid of cells at least 10.5rem wide.

Public pages keep the 56px Prussian header and the `max-w-7xl` container (24px side padding from `sm`). The landing hero is a 12-column grid filling the first viewport on a graticule ground: copy in five columns on the left, the season chart bleeding to the right edge across 58% of the width. Sections are separated by full-width hairline bands (`border-y`) and 64–112px of vertical padding. Auth pages split 5:6 on large screens: the Prussian panel with graticule left, the form (max 24rem) right.

Breakpoints are Tailwind's defaults (640, 768, 1024, 1280px). Spacing follows the 4px grid; between data cells the gap is the line itself, 1px between soundings and 3px between calendar days.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth is tonal and literal: the depth tints carry occupancy, the Prussian bar sits as the darkest field, shoal days recede below chart-water days. Separation comes from hairlines and from the grid-line color showing through the gaps between cells, not from shadows.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Control hairline** (`shadow-xs`): outline buttons, inputs and native selects, so a control reads as pressable.
- **Overlay** (`shadow-lg`, shadcn default): sheets, popovers and menus that float above the page.
- **Chart frame** (`0 30px 60px -30px var(--frame-shadow)`, Prussian-tinted `rgb(8 19 29 / 0.22)` in light, `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55)` in dark): the landing's screenshot frame and closing call-to-action card only.

### Named Rules

**The Line-Before-Shadow Rule.** Group with a grid line, a hairline or a tonal field first. A shadow appears only where something floats (overlays) or on the landing's two framed objects. Stay bars carry no shadow.

## Shapes

Corners are small and printed. The radius base is 6px (`--radius: 0.375rem`), with 4px for small elements, 6px for controls, sounding strips, the calendar frame and the brand tile, and 8px for bordered data regions (tables, lists, the financial summary, empty and error states). Inside the chart, corners shrink further: calendar day cells are 2px, stay bars, day numbers and landing thumbnails 3px. The key dot on a sounding is a 2px-rounded square ringed in card white. Fully round only for badges and the empty-state icon disc; the avatar stays square-cornered at 6px. A stay bar that continues past a week row loses its rounded edge on that side, so a long stay reads as one course. Empty states use a dashed border; error states a destructive-tinted border.

## Components

### Top bar and tabs

- **Field:** `bar` background, `bar-foreground` text, a `bar-border` hairline below; sticky, 56px.
- **Brand:** a 32px 6px-radius Carolina tile with a wide extrabold "H" over a 1px waterline, beside "Hostly" in wide bold 1.125rem. It links home and is never marked current.
- **Tabs:** Reservas · Imóveis · Intermediadores, each an icon and 14px medium label, 12px side padding, full bar height. Inactive in `bar-muted`, hover to `bar-foreground`; active in `bar-foreground` with a 2px Carolina underline that scales in from the left (500ms, ease-out-expo). Focus is an inset 2px Carolina ring.
- **Right side:** theme toggle as a ghost icon button (`bar-accent` hover) and the user menu: a 32px avatar with initials on `bar-accent`, `bar-border` stroke, opening a dropdown with the name, a theme submenu and "Sair".
- **Progress:** a 2px Carolina bar at the very top while a route loads.

### Occupancy soundings (signature)

- One cell per property in a 6px-radius strip whose 1px gaps show the grid line. Each cell is filled with the depth tint for its share of booked nights and its paired foreground, 12px padding, at least 96px tall.
- Top: the property's ink dot and name (14px medium, truncated). Bottom: the night count as a wide tabular sounding and an italic "de N noites".
- Above the strip: "Ocupação em {mês}" (14px semibold) and an italic water line explaining the key.
- Each cell is a toggle that shows that property alone on the calendar: pressed gets an inset 2px ring; the others drop to 55% opacity (80% on hover).
- Cells fill with the tide on load and again on every month change, staggered by 70ms.

### Bookings calendar

The calendar's structure is fixed: a seven-column month or week grid, a toolbar (Hoje, previous/next ghost icon buttons, the wide month title, the Mês|Semana toggle), stays as bars in lanes, 3 to 5 lanes per week in the month view with "+N reservas" opening the week, and click-or-drag on free days to start a booking. Change its surface, not its structure.

- **Frame:** a 6px-radius block filled with `grid-line`, 3px padding and 3px gaps, so the grid lines are the background showing through.
- **Weekday header:** card-white cells, 2px radius, 12px medium uppercase with wide tracking, muted.
- **Days:** month days are chart water (card fill with contour lines fixed to the viewport so they run on across cells); other months' days are `shoal` with muted numbers. The day number sits in a 24px square, 3px radius; today is filled `primary`. Hover tints the day with `accent` at 40%; the drag-selected range is `accent` with an inset 2px `primary` ring.

### Stay bars

- 20px tall, 3px radius, 8px side padding, 12px semibold, in the property's ink with its foreground; 85% fill on hover. Inset 3px from the day edges; square on the side where the stay continues into the next week row.
- The guest name, then the property name where the bar has room. Focus is a 2px ring with a 1px offset.
- Bars are traced along their nights on load, staggered by lane and column.

### Buttons

- **Shape:** 6px radius; 36px default, 32px small, 40px large (44px for the landing name field's submit), square icon sizes.
- **Primary:** `primary` with its foreground, 16px side padding, 14px medium; 90% fill on hover.
- **Outline:** `background` fill, hairline border, `shadow-xs`; `accent` on hover. Used for Hoje, retry and secondary actions.
- **Ghost:** no fill, `accent` on hover; calendar navigation and row icon actions.
- **On the bar:** ghost buttons use `bar-foreground` with `bar-accent` hover; the primary is Carolina (`bar-active`) with `depth-4-foreground` text.
- **Focus:** border turns `ring` with a 3px ring at 50%.

### Inputs

- Transparent fill (30% `input` at night), `input` stroke, 6px radius, 36px tall, 12px side padding, `shadow-xs`. Native selects (property filter, form selects) copy the same recipe.
- **Focus:** `ring` border and a 3px `ring` halo at 50%. **Error:** destructive border and ring on `aria-invalid`. **Disabled:** 50% opacity.

### Sheets

- Booking, property and broker forms open in a right-side sheet: `background` fill, left hairline, `shadow-lg`, full width on phones and up to 36rem from `sm` (32rem for brokers), scrolling inside, sliding in. The booking sheet ends with the financial summary: a muted 50% field in an 8px bordered region, tabular rows, and "Falta receber" as a wide 1.5rem `primary` figure ("Quitado" when paid).

### Tables, lists and badges

- Data lives in 8px bordered regions with hairline row dividers; documents in the system mono at 12px. Badges are the secondary pill (Azure fill, 12px medium) for type labels and states.

### Landing season chart

- January drawn as the app draws it: a soundings strip of four cells (three sample properties and the visitor's), above a 7×6 grid with 1px grid-line gaps, chart-water January days and shoal days of December and February, and every stay traced in its property's ink as a thin 14px band.
- Hovering or focusing a sounding lights its property's bands and dims the rest to 20%. The name the visitor types becomes a property in ink 5 with a first stay on free nights, ringed in that ink.
- It sits on the hero's graticule ground, bleeding off the right edge with a 6px left corner.

### Motion

- **Surface** (600ms, ease-out-expo): from 40% opacity and 6px lower to rest. Page titles, the hero copy, the auth form, calendar days (staggered 30ms), the "Falta receber" figure.
- **Tide** (1100ms, ease-out-expo): a sounding fills left to right from a visible 8% sliver. Sounding cells, staggered 70–90ms.
- **Trace** (900ms, ease-out-expo): a stay is drawn along its nights by a clip from left to right. Stay bars and landing bands, staggered.
- **Reduced motion:** under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, every animation and transition collapses to 1ms with no delay, so everything appears in its final state.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** take every color from a theme token and check it in light and dark.
- **Do** use the depth tints only for occupancy, through `depthOf`, with each step's paired foreground.
- **Do** keep each property's ink fixed across the calendar, the soundings key and the lists.
- **Do** keep the brand on the Prussian bar, with Carolina as its only action color.
- **Do** set titles wide (`font-wide`) and secondary explanatory labels italic (`font-water`), and use tabular figures for money, dates and soundings.
- **Do** separate data with grid lines and hairlines; let the grid-line color show through the gaps.
- **Do** start motion from a visible state with ease-out-expo, and let reduced motion turn it off.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use a serif, or any face other than Archivo Variable.
- **Don't** add decorative map ornament: no compass roses, anchors, rope, waves, ships or illustrated maps. The chart is depth tints, contour lines, graticule and soundings.
- **Don't** put the graticule anywhere but chart surfaces: the landing hero ground and the auth panel. Contour water belongs to the calendar's month days.
- **Don't** change the bookings calendar's structure; change only its surface.
- **Don't** use depth tints or property inks for status, emphasis or decoration.
- **Don't** write loose hex or rgb values in components.
- **Don't** add a sidebar; sections are tabs on the top bar.
- **Don't** reach for a shadow where a line or a tonal field would separate the content.
